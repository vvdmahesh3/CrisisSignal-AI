🚨 How CrisisSignal Works

A simple guide — from reporting to resolution in 5 steps

🏢
Step 1 — Join Your Building
Every building or campus is a Community. When you register, you either create a new community (you become the admin) or join an existing one using a 6-letter code. Users only see alerts from their own community — like a private group.
📝
Step 2 — Report an Incident
See something wrong? Click Report Incident. Write what happened in plain words. Add the location and an optional photo. Hit Submit — the AI reads your message in under 1 second and automatically decides the type (fire, medical, theft...), severity (1–10), and how confident it is.
👥
Step 3 — Your Community Verifies It
Other members in your building see the alert under "Awaiting Verification". They click ✅ Confirm ("Yes, I see it too") or ❌ Reject ("Nothing here, false alarm"). Trusted users' votes count more — a security guard's confirmation carries more weight than a random newcomer.
📈
Step 4 — The Alert Evolves Automatically
As votes arrive, the Confidence Score updates live. The alert moves through stages:
🔵 New → 🟡 Verifying → 🟢 Verified → 🔴 Critical → ✔️ Resolved
If confidence + severity are both very high, the system auto-escalates to Critical — no admin action needed.
🛡️
Step 5 — Admin Acts, System Learns
The Building Admin sees all alerts live — sorted by priority. They can Escalate, Resolve, or Assign a security guard. After resolution, your Reliability Score updates: accurate reporters and voters gain trust, pranksters lose influence over time. Everything is recorded in an Audit Log — nothing is ever lost.
